Decision in force

Seat capacity

What work is allowed to consume Derek's time at the keyboard?

Only a prepared batch of authored choices, spatial judgment, visual review, narrative, responsiveness, and play feel. Deployment, retries, relays, capture transport, and machine proof belong to automation.Source
Decision in force

Definition of proof

What evidence decides whether a feature is ready?

Automated installed vertical-slice journeys first, boundary integration second, focused unit tests third. Report crossed boundaries and retained evidence rather than test totals.Source
Decision in force

Runtime surface

Does the overhead bar replace the old F9 drawer?

Yes. F9 expands or minimizes one always-present overhead bar; a single clamped alert anchor carries deadline or actionable warning state.Source
Decision in force

Playable world authority

Does Godbuild generation replace the saved guild world?

No. The versioned saved world owns terrain, vegetation, portal topology, and authored spatial context. Godbuild capture is optional reusable module/source/diff authority only for its declared fields.Source
Decision in force

World-lap routing

Which world should mechanics development and installed integration use?

Use ComfyQuestDemo and questyfour by default. Promote the same immutable content/package bytes to ComfyEra17 only for retained-world compatibility, scale/load/save, actual topology, spatial pacing, or batched human acceptance; never migrate run state between them.Source
Decision in force

Guild ownership hierarchy

Who configures a Guild, who creates with it, and what proves the first abstraction?

A community steward owns Guild configuration, palette, and progression; creators instantiate its abstractions into artifacts and campaigns; players receive the resulting experiences. Prove that hierarchy first with one typed Slayers Signature Hunt abstraction, two distinct creator instances, one campaign, and live evidence — not a generic framework.Source
Decision in force

R&D versus distribution

Does versioned world packaging belong on the active R&D path?

No. Use recoverable local world copies while authoring stabilizes the contracts. Build `.db`/`.fwl` packaging only when community distribution becomes the next result.Source
Decision in force

Development MCP boundary

May the turnkey Workbench borrow Derek's private HEARTH gateway or fleet topology?

Absolutely not. Use an independently released, identity-attested Isolate profile with generated local credentials and no private fallback, path, host, state, or provider coupling.Source
Decision in force

Fleet roles

When should the nearby i5 peer be powered on?

Only when the multiplayer adapter changes. Content-only and spatial work stay on the Creator Session lane without a human relay between machines.Source

Guardrails

  • Golden rule: do not call Derek into the seat to discover, relay, or retry anything the fleet can build, drive, observe, capture, diagnose, or close itself.
  • A visible client needed only for machine work belongs on the unattended automation seat. Keep OMEN released until the remaining question actually requires Derek's perception or authored choice; focus theft and accidental tabbing are seat costs.
  • A passing unit suite proves neither integration nor usability. Drive the installed Studio-to-Valheim journey and retain its real evidence before promoting a feature.
  • Never connect by port alone. The development gateway must attest the exact standalone Isolate release and must have no HEARTH, private-machine, or sibling-checkout coupling.
  • The Creator Session lease owns the shared Valheim installation. Do not run another deployment or Lab mutation beside it.
  • Run mechanics and integration on ComfyQuestDemo first. ERA17 is acceptance/scale-only and must not become the default iteration world merely because it holds the target creative experience.
  • Rehearsal declares its own proof level, disclaimer, and limitations; it does not prove Unity, BepInEx, Harmony, or live mutation.
  • A screenshot or checkbox is orientation, not machine proof. Preserve request receipts, identity pins, capture hashes, and MATCH.
  • Close -Restore restores prior install bytes plus quarantined one-shot world-entry request/status state and retains authored world state. After Stop, a disposable technical lap may add -RestoreGameState to restore the exact pinned world pair and character profile; this bounded rollback is not saved-world packaging.
  • The installed 4A journey is proven and its mechanical defects were fixed and rerun before the seat. Do not reopen deployment, lifecycle, binding, reset, retention, or recovery as human steps; a 4B seat turn is for authored choices and perception only.
  • A simulated `comfy-quest-studio-campaign-play/v1` receipt in state `started` can close the declared fixture, target, instance, machine/world/session, successor, and applied-binding identity chain through bind/start. Fixture preparation is still not kill proof, bind/start is still not successor completion or campaign-completion proof, and the installed 4B lap remains open.
  • Receipts are authority, not logs. Retention archives rather than deletes, and the archive bound writes a `receipt_archive_evicted` receipt when it destroys anything — so a gap in a correlated proof set is a fact you can read, never something to infer from absence.
